The assumption-independent signal: demand is outrunning output

Jan 2024 · 125/moMonthly referralsMay 2026 · 274/mo

Referrals rose ~65% while monthly outreach output plateaued near 800–1,000. Rising input against flat output is the defining signature of a capacity-constrained team — true regardless of the levers above.

Bottom line

The team is at its ceiling. The per diem pool adds a modest buffer, not structural headroom. Closing the gap between rising demand and flat output requires added core FTE, not additional per diem shifts.

Capacity = (core FTE + per diem FTE-equivalent) × productive hours × patients/hr, less clinic reservation.
