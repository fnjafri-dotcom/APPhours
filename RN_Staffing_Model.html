Confirm before this goes to leadership
  1. Are the 1,650 IP / 6,200 ED baselines truly raw volumes?
  2. Non-eligible patients aren't pre-screened off the roster — nurses review the chart and document in EPIC without dialing out. That work earns EPIC credit but no WebEx credit, and is part of why the two systems disagree. Counted here at 3–5 min/chart (per Michelle); toggle off to see calls only.
  3. Supply reflects the actual roster — 6 FT + 1 PT + 5 per diem at the hours they truly work (216.8 hrs/wk). Per diem is counted as worked, not as an assumed FTE-equivalent.
  4. No salary/loaded-cost data yet — the dollar impact still needs Finance rates.
  5. Non-outreach hours are added once for the whole team, but the source labels them "per RN FTE." At 18.7 flat that is ~2.6 hrs/wk per nurse for all huddle, documentation, escalation and admin combined. Worth confirming — see note below.
