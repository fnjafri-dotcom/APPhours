The assumption-independent signal: demand is outrunning output

Jan 2024 · 125/moMonthly referralsMay 2026 · 274/mo

Referral demand rose ~65% (125–160/mo in early 2024 → 250–274/mo in early 2026), while monthly outreach output has plateaued near 800–1,000. Rising input against flat output is the defining signature of a capacity-constrained team — true regardless of which per-hour assumption is used.

Bottom line

The team is at its ceiling. The 3 per diems (6 shifts/month) add only ~0.4 FTE and ~17 patients/week — a useful margin, but not enough to absorb sustained referral growth. Closing the gap between rising demand and flat output requires added core FTE, not additional per diem shifts. The clinic/TCM coverage split further constrains available outreach capacity below the 4-FTE nominal figure.
